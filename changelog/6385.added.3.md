- Added standard deliveries and sounds to expressive conversation (alpha), `STANDARD_DELIVERY` and `STANDARD_SOUNDS`, for a processor that writes tags from a fixed set of labels, and `BREATH_KINDS`, the kinds such a processor draws a breath as: a light breath, a deep breath or an exhale. `ExpressionCapabilities.standard_names` lists the names a service performs each label and kind by, best first, filled in for Cartesia, ElevenLabs, Fish Audio and MiniMax, and `standard_name()` returns the first one the service performs and an expression policy offers, or None.
